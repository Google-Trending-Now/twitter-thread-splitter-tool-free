Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on character limits, manual splits, and how your drafts are handled.

Is this Twitter thread splitter tool free to use without an account?

Yes. ThreadSplit is a twitter thread splitter tool free for everyone, with no signup, no paywall, and no daily post limits. Open the page, paste your draft, and copy your finished posts.

How does the X post character counter thread engine count links and emojis?

Every URL is counted as exactly 23 characters, matching how X wraps links through t.co regardless of how long the original web address is. Standard emojis, compound emojis like family icons or flags, and CJK characters each count as 2 characters, matching X's official counting rules.

Will ThreadSplit ever cut a word in half between two posts?

No. In Smart Auto mode, the splitter looks for paragraph breaks first, then full sentences, and finally spaces between words. It keeps every word whole and reserves space for your thread numbering and optional thread emoji so posts stay under the limit.

Can I control exactly where each post breaks?

Yes. You can place --- on its own line anywhere in Smart Auto mode to force a break, or switch to Blank Lines or Manual --- mode for full control. If a manual section goes over your character limit, the post counter turns red and tells you how many characters to trim.

Where is my draft stored? Do you save my writing on a server?

Your writing stays on your own device. ThreadSplit runs entirely inside your browser and saves your draft and settings to localStorage so you will not lose work if you refresh the tab.
